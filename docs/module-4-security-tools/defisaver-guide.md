# DeFi Saver – Automated Position Management

**DeFi Saver** automates your Compound, Aave, or Maker positions by watching your health ratio and executing predefined triggers like auto-repay or leverage boosts.

---

## What’s Happening in the Demo

The GIF shows a user:

- Accessing their DeFi portfolio.
- Viewing real-time supply, borrow, and collateral ratios.
- Enabling automated actions to respond to liquidation risk.

---

## Key Features

- **Auto-repay**: DeFi Saver repays a portion of your debt if collateralization drops below a threshold.
- **Auto-boost**: In bullish conditions, it can increase leverage by borrowing more safely.
- **Full control**: Parameters are configurable; all execution happens via smart contracts — no key custody.

---

**Best for:** Users managing leveraged positions, or sleeping through market volatility.
