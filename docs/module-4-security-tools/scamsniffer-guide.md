# Scam Sniffer – Real-Time Phishing Defense

**Scam Sniffer** is a browser extension that instantly blocks phishing sites, drainer scripts, and fake popups before they interact with your wallet.

---

## What It Shows

This demo showcases Scam Sniffer:

- Intercepting a malicious site.
- Flagging it clearly as a phishing attempt.
- Preventing you from interacting with the page until dismissed.

---

## Why It’s Powerful

- **Real-time detection** based on a large and frequently updated blocklist.
- Recognizes patterns common to wallet drainers, fake DApps, and impersonation popups.
- Works inside your browser silently until triggered — no extra clicks needed.

**Best for:** All users, especially those using Discord, Telegram, and search engines to discover dApps.
