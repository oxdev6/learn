# Impact of Market Volatility on Borrowing Positions

Market volatility—large price swings—can rapidly change your loan-to-value (LTV) ratio and push you toward liquidation.

---

## How Volatility Affects Collateral Value

- **Collateral Value Drop:** If your collateral asset’s price falls, your collateral value decreases, raising your LTV.  
- **Debt Stability:** If you borrowed a stablecoin, your debt amount remains constant in USD, while collateral value fluctuates.

---

## LTV Changes with Price Swings

- **Example:**  
  - Collateral: 2 ETH at \$2,000/ETH → \$4,000  
  - Borrowed: \$1,500 USDC → LTV = 37.5%  
  - ETH price drops 30% to \$1,400 → Collateral = \$2,800  
  - New LTV = \$1,500 / \$2,800 ≈ 53.6%  

---

## Sudden vs. Gradual Moves

- **Sudden Crashes:** Can trigger liquidations before you can react.  
- **Gradual Declines:** Give you time to self-liquidate or add collateral.

---

## Correlation and Diversification

- **Correlated Collateral:** Supplying multiple assets that move together offers limited risk reduction.  
- **Diversification Caution:** Assets often decline together in broad market downturns.

---

## Managing Volatility Risk

1. **Leave a Cushion:** Borrow at a lower LTV (e.g., 40–50%).  
2. **Use Alerts:** Price alerts for your collateral’s critical price levels.  
3. **Have Liquidity on Hand:** Keep stablecoins ready to repay debt.  
4. **Consider Hedges:** Use options or other derivatives to offset sharp declines.
