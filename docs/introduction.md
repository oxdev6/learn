## What Is Compound v3?

Compound v3 (also called “Comet”) is an over-collateralized, single-borrow-asset DeFi lending protocol. Users supply crypto assets as collateral and borrow a stablecoin (e.g., USDC) against it. Proper risk management is crucial to avoid liquidation penalties and potential losses.

---

## How This Site Is Organized

LearnSecure is divided into **four core modules**, each focusing on a key aspect of DeFi security within Compound v3:

1. **Module 1: Safe Borrowing Practices**  
   Learn how collateral factors and loan-to-value (LTV) ratios work, plus strategies to avoid over-leveraging and self-liquidate before it’s too late.

2. **Module 2: Liquidation Risk Management**  
   Dive into Compound’s liquidation mechanics, see how market volatility affects your positions, and discover early-warning tools to stay ahead of the curve.

3. **Module 3: Scam Prevention**  
   Identify common DeFi scams—phishing sites, malicious contracts, permission exploits—and learn how to verify that you’re interacting with the real Compound protocol.

4. **Module 4: Security Tools & Best Practices**  
   Explore hardware wallets, smart contract scanners, transaction simulators, and tokens-approval management to build a robust defense around your assets.
